How do LLMs express themselves musically?

Each piece was generated once by a language model, asked only to express itself — written either as ABC (a compact plain-text music notation) or as Python code that builds the score (“code-gen”). Notation is engraved (typeset) and played live in your browser with Verovio and abcjs. The experimental design descends from sara-fish/llm-musical-self-expression.
